          Equipment Reliability — Availability, MTBF & MTTR

          Enterprise controls in this build

          • Role-based access — admin, reliability, operations, executive, viewer with menu and action gating
          • Audit trail — actor, action, timestamp, equipment context on critical writes
          • Data residency indicator — South Africa primary; TLS in transit via Firebase
          • Reliability layer — Event-based risk scores, gated MTBF/MTTR, data-quality metrics (RUL deferred until calibrated models exist)
          • Export & governance — Executive PDF/Excel for board and reliability reviews

          Roadmap toward Emerson / ABB / AVEVA class

          Near term

          IoT/sensor connectors, historian API, webhook alerts, SSO (SAML/OIDC)

          Mid term

          Calibrated reliability models, multi-site hierarchy, CMMS/ERP bi-directional sync (Phase 3–4)

          Strategic

          Digital twins, edge agents, global support org, formal SOC 2 / ISO program

          Support channels

          For production deployment: designate a platform owner, define RACI for reliability vs operations, and route critical alerts to on-call.
