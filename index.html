          Text Mining Framework – Predictive Maintenance

          This panel implements the text-mining framework described in the research proposal (Development of a Text Mining Framework for Predictive Maintenance of Underground Locomotives). Unstructured artisan notes (free-text Reason/Fault from status updates and imported Excel breakdown reports) are classified with a rule-based text classifier — the same approach used in the study results — into engineering failure modes. No additional sensors are required. Results update automatically when new records are logged or imported.
